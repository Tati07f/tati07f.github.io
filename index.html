About

Hi, I'm Tatiana.

I'm a Calgary-based graphic designer with a background in fine art and more than thirteen years of design work for agencies, in-house marketing teams and my own freelance clients. I've designed everything from menus and brochures to billboards and vehicle graphics, and I often photograph and retouch the images that go into them.

I like organized files, clear brand rules and work that holds together across every channel. I'm used to juggling several projects at once, presenting ideas and turning feedback into finished work quickly.
